Assembles the single-session security bound (uniformStartMixtureBound) for the Double Ratchet with SPQR V1 (paramsV1) from the ACD19 (Alwen, Coretti, Dodis, EUROCRYPT 2019) Fig. 8 authenticity and privacy bounds (fig8MixShortAuthV1, fig8MixShortPrivV1, fsAeadMixtureBound), the session-to-Fig. 8 refinement slack (refineConst, hiddenSaltMixtureBound; DEV-LS36), and the epoch-0 and uniform-start root-KDF PRG switches (eps0IdealUnit). Also defines the early-corruption refusal wrapper RefuseB0.refuseCorrB0 (DEV-LS33, DEV-LS34), ML-KEM encapsulation totality (Encaps1Total), and scalar-independent DH equality fibers (DhKerUniform).

Bounds are stated over a context mixture oa : ProbComp α via prefixPrgAvg, prefixPrfAvg, prefixWireAvg, and prefixAdvAvg, and evaluated on first-order ReductionDsl.CostedReduction bundles (SessionCostedReductions, sessionCostedReductionsBounded; see Computational/docs/REDUCTION_DSL_TUTORIAL.md).
